R&D Career in Korea

When settling in becomes easier, research shines brighter.

We support international science & technology researchers through the entire journey in Korea — from starting graduate school to employment, settlement, and career development.

KOLAB, a new one-stop platform for international researchers, launches in early October!

Starting then, all applications for programs under the Full-Cycle Settlement Support Program for International Researchers will move to KOLAB.

Beyond program applications, KOLAB brings together everything you need to research and settle in Korea — from practical settlement guides and career development resources to field-specific consulting — all in one place.
